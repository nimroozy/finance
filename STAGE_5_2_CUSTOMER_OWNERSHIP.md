# Stage 5.2 — Permanent customer ownership and branch Zoho account mapping

Implements permanent collector ownership separate from temporary assignments, recurring invoice auto-routing into a customer work queue, branch Zoho cash/bank account mapping with readiness gates, and immediate Zoho payment posting using the branch account. Handover remains internal custody and does not create a second Zoho customer payment.
